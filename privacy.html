Privacy and advertising

Updated October 10, 2026.

Game data

Some games and tools store progress, preferences, and game-open counts in your browser using local storage. Clearing your browser's site data can remove saved progress.

Advertising

Daft Arcade uses Google AdSense. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, device information, IP addresses, and similar technologies to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Google advertising cookies can enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and manage cookies through your browser settings. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and online features

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and connected online services may process technical information needed to deliver pages, handle requests, and secure their services. Online multiplayer and connected AI tools send the information needed for those features to their configured services. Avoid entering private or sensitive information into public game sessions or AI tools.
