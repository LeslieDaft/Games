ENDLESS RHYTHM CHALLENGE

How long can you hold the beat?

Start with slow quarter notes. Eighth notes, rests, doubles and sixteenths join gradually as the tempo climbs. Follow the L/R sticking under the music. Misses and extra hits drain your groove.

A four-beat count-in gets you ready. Sound recommended.

01 — Read ahead

Music flows right to left. Filled notes with plain stems are one beat; one flag is half a beat, two flags a quarter. Rest marks mean silence.

02 — Two hands, real rhythm

Use ← / → or F / J. Every press is one snare strike; holding a key won’t roll. You can also tap the two pads.

03 — Stay in the pocket

Precise hits restore groove. New patterns arrive every four bars. Tempo rises 1 BPM each bar, from 70 to 180. Best survival time saves on this device.
